Professional Summary

María Inés Dávalos Vázquez is the founder and director of Dance Studio Maseuani, a bilingual dance and movement studio based in the East Bay of the San Francisco Bay Area, California. With a background in Performing Arts with a concentration in Folkloric Dance from the Universidad de Guadalajara and over a decade of teaching experience, she has developed a comprehensive approach that blends traditional Mexican dance with therapeutic, disability-informed methodology. Her work is driven by a personal motivation — disability within her own family — which led her to build culturally relevant, genuinely accessible programming for schools, community organizations, and Spanish-speaking families.
